Thorcin Pantheon

 5th June 2026 at 1:43pm
DeitiesPublicSetting

The Thorcinga have a remarkably diverse pantheon, especially for a people that have never built a lasting monumental civilization. If Father Utar is the technical head of the pantheon, he is in fact often an object of ridicule for the failure of his (and Thorcin) schemes.
