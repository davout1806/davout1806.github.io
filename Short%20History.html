Short History

PublicSetting

Roughly 2800 years ago the Archontean empire took hold of the world. The Archonteans unified the human cultures of their home island and enslaved the goblins of the same island. For the next several centuries, the Archonteans expanded across the Winedark Sea. Eventually this expansion brought the Archonteans – in the form of the heroes Arden and Vul - to discover the cliff now bearing their names. A city was established on the cliff face 2,100 years ago and the Archonteans began exploring the caverns below.

During this golden age, the Archonteans delved deeply beneath the cliff, erecting tombs, temples, shrines, and administrative chambers therein. Ultimately rivalries within the empire, between the conservative forces known as the Thesosophs and radical egalitarians known as Sortians, broke into open conflict. The ensuing civil war crippled the Archontean empire and the city of Arden Vul was sacked 1,210 years ago. Twenty years later, the emperor ordered the formal abandonment of Arden Vul and the withdrawal of all remaining legions from Irthuin.

A dark age for Archontos set in, and though the empire crumbled, it did not fall. Some 300 years ago the Archonteans returned in muted triumph to Irthuin. A formal Archontean expedition to reclaim Arden Vul was dispatched 250 years ago, but within a decade it had foundered. It seems the former Jewel of Irthuin will not so easily be reconquered.
